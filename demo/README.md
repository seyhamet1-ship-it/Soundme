## 3. 60-Second Live Demo Script

### Timeline & Action Guide

- **00:00 – 00:10 | Introduction & Setup**
  - **Action:** Open `http://localhost:5173` in your browser.
  - **Say:** *"Welcome to Soundme, a local-first AI studio that automatically dubs Chinese and English videos into natural Khmer speech and subtitles."*

- **00:10 – 00:25 | Upload & Configuration**
  - **Action:** Drag and drop `sample_input.mp4` (or any short video) into the dropzone. Select **Voice: Sreymom (Female)**, toggle **Keep Background Music** ON, toggle **Burn Subtitles** ON, and check **Review Before TTS**. Click **Start Dubbing**.
  - **Say:** *"We support customizable options including male and female Khmer voices, background audio ducking, burned-in Khmer OS typography, and human-in-the-loop transcript review."*

- **00:25 – 00:40 | Interactive Review Modal**
  - **Action:** When the progress reaches the Review stage, the modal pops up showing the English ASR transcript on the left and the editable Khmer translation on the right. Click the speaker icon to preview the live Khmer voice synthesis.
  - **Say:** *"Here, Soundme presents the transcribed and translated segments. You can edit any Khmer word in real-time, test the audio preview with one click, and approve the synthesis."*

- **00:40 – 00:55 | Dual Player & Verification**
  - **Action:** Click **Approve & Dub**. Watch the final mixing complete. When the results screen appears, press Play on the dual player.
  - **Say:** *"In just seconds, our two-stage rate-fitting engine syncs the Khmer audio to the speaker's original cadence. Notice how the background music ducks smoothly behind the Khmer voice."*

- **00:55 – 01:00 | Export Options**
  - **Action:** Point out the download buttons: **Download Dubbed MP4**, **Download Audio (WAV)**, **Download Subtitles (.srt / .vtt)**.
  - **Say:** *"You can immediately download the finished video, separate studio audio, or standardized subtitles ready for distribution. Thank you!"*
